# v0.2.0 — Identity

**Released:** 2026-09-17

A business registers a product and receives a TrustPass ID. One physical product
cannot hold two live identities.

This page describes only what the `v0.2.0` tag contains. Later work belongs in
[`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md) under Unreleased.

## What's new

### Identity

- **TrustPass ID** — `TP1-` followed by 128 bits from the CSPRNG in Crockford
  base32, plus a check symbol. Every single-character typo is detectable, which
  means "you mistyped this" is a distinct answer from "this product is not
  registered". Telling someone at a counter that the product they are buying is
  unregistered, when they fat-fingered one character, is a false fraud
  accusation.
  ([ADR 0004](../adr/0004-trustpass-id-format.md))
- **Issuer model** — four verification states, because "we hold their legal
  registration", "we hold nothing" and "we verified them and later revoked it"
  are different facts. New issuers default to `unverified`, which is the honest
  default.
- **Product model** — the record a TrustPass ID resolves to. The issuer foreign
  key is `RESTRICT`: deleting an issuer would erase the provenance of everything
  it signed.

### Lifecycle

- **Status transitions enforced by a database trigger**, not by application
  code, because application code is bypassed by the first write path that does
  not call it.
- `retired` is terminal, and retiring a product is what **frees its serial** so
  a warranty replacement can reuse it.
- `suspended` cannot return to `active` in one step. A product that quietly
  becomes active again erases the reason it was suspended.
  ([Lifecycle](../product-lifecycle.md))

### Registration

- **`POST /products`** — validation at the boundary, an identifier generated
  server side and never read from the request, one documented error shape, and
  the endpoint published in the OpenAPI document generated from the same schemas
  that validate.
- **One live identity per serial, per issuer** — a partial unique index over
  issuer and lower-cased serial. A duplicate returns `409` naming the TrustPass
  ID the serial already belongs to, so a client that timed out and retried can
  recover rather than guess.

### Engineering

- CodeQL, dependency review and a weekly full-history secret scan
- A risk-based merge policy: `low` and `medium` merge on green gates, `high` and
  `critical` wait for a human
- `pnpm db:reset`, which removes the volume — `DROP SCHEMA public CASCADE` leaves
  Drizzle's ledger behind and is not a reset

## Known limitations

- **No authentication.** Anyone who can reach `POST /products` can register
  against any issuer, and can learn the TrustPass ID of a product they did not
  register by guessing a serial and reading the 409. **This must not be exposed
  publicly before `TP-141`.**
- No public passport page, warranty, lifecycle events or ownership transfer.
- Serials are stored whole and are not safe to display. Masking is `TP-031`.
- Nothing records *why* a status changed. That is `TP-050`, and until it exists
  a suspension is enforceable but unexplainable.
- Not deployed. Local only.
